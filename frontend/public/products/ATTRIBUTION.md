# Product photo credits

The ten sample products in `database/migrations/003_seed.sql` point at these files, served
from `frontend/public/products/` as `/products/<file>`. They are Wikimedia Commons images,
resized by Commons to about 900 px wide and stored here so the storefront needs no
external host and the demo keeps working offline.

Licences below are the ones recorded on each file's Commons page. CC BY and CC BY-SA
require attribution (this file) and, for BY-SA, that a modified version is shared under
the same licence — the files here are unmodified copies of the Commons thumbnails.
