1280 × 850. It replaced the 21st.dev sample image, which carried no stated licence.

The three testimonial avatars come from the
[SignInPage](https://21st.dev/@bhomikproductivitylab/components/sign-in-page) template by
Bhomik and are stored locally so the login page makes no third-party image request. They
belong to invented testimonials, so replace them together with that copy before a real
launch.

Keeping `login-hero.jpg` requires the credit above to stay reachable from the project. If
the attribution is inconvenient, swap in a CC0 photo instead.
